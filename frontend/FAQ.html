Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about using and booking the Student Center.

Any currently enrolled University of Houston student can use the open lounge areas and book rooms. Faculty and staff may book rooms for student-facing events. Guests are welcome when accompanied by a student.

Use the Book a Room page. Choose a room, date, start time and how long you need it. You’ll see right away whether the room is free, and you’ll get a confirmation after you submit.

Bookings can last 1 to 3 hours and can be made up to 60 days in advance. Each booking must end before the Student Center closes for the day.

Please cancel at least 2 hours ahead so another student can use the room. If you haven’t checked in within 15 minutes of your start time, the room may be released to walk-ins.

Yes. Free UH Wi-Fi covers the whole building, and light snacks and drinks are allowed in every room except the Media & Podcast Studio. Park in any student lot with a valid UH parking permit.
